Greater Manchester housing and council tax analysis

Analysis of official open data from HM Land Registry and the Valuation Office Agency across Greater Manchester's 10 boroughs. I filtered 90,867 national property sales down to 2,964 standard Greater Manchester sales, and loaded 32 yearly snapshots of housing stock (1993–2024), finding and fixing a double-counting bug along the way.

Problem
How do house prices and housing stock differ across Greater Manchester's 10 boroughs?
What I did
Filtered 90,867 national property sales from HM Land Registry down to 2,964 standard Greater Manchester sales, and loaded 32 yearly housing-stock snapshots (1993–2024) from the Valuation Office Agency, finding and fixing a double-counting bug along the way.
Result
Trafford's median sale price (£352,000) is almost twice Wigan's (£183,248), the lowest of the 10 boroughs. Housing stock grew from about 1.07 million homes in 1993 to over 1.28 million in 2024, and Manchester has the most homes, at about 249,300.

Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Scikit-learn · Jupyter
